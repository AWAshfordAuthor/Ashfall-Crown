The Story

Some lies survive longer than the people who tell them.

Fourteen years after Caelan Voss let his younger brother fall into the darkness beneath Mount Kestren, he has built a life mapping ruins and avoiding the past.

Then a dying courier carves Caelan’s name into stone and delivers an impossible warning: the crown remembers you.

Drawn toward the capital of Velis, Caelan must face a crystal creature wearing his brother’s face, a sovereign willing to sacrifice anything to save his people, and the truth behind the ash that has hidden humanity for thousands of years.

“The Ashfall was not falling.
It was rising.”
